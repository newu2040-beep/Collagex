CollageX Preview is a modern photo collage creation app that lets users combine multiple photos into beautiful, customizable layouts. It offers flexible grids, creative templates, adjustable borders, backgrounds, spacing, and aspect ratios.
Users can edit their collages and resize the finished canvas using preset aspect ratios or custom width and height values in pixels, inches, and other units while preserving editable photos and layout elements.

3. Collage Studio & Editing Features
Layouts & Capacity: 2 to 50+ photos across Grid, Hero (Asymmetric), Masonry, Bento, Strip, Mosaic, and Freeform layouts.
Auto-Fit Studio: Automatically organizes layout, spacing, borders, and corner radius to match the selected ratio and canvas size, while keeping all manual edits unlocked.
Delete & Don't Delete Layer Protection: Easily delete photos, text, and stickers—or protect individual layers with Don't Delete to prevent accidental removal.
Filters & Adjustments: 12 filters plus Sharpen, Vignette, Brightness, Exposure, Contrast, Highlights, Shadows, Saturation, Vibrance, Warmth, Tint, and Fade/Matte controls.
Themes & Fonts: 12 color themes with System/Light/Dark modes, plus bundled Google Fonts and Android system fonts.
Custom Canvas Sizing: Resize non-destructively in px, in, cm, mm, or pt with custom DPI, Fit/Fill/Crop/Extend modes, and live preview.
